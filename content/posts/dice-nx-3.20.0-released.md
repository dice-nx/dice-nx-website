The first release of the DICE fork and reboot, DICE-nx, is now available for download.

DICE was a C compiler suite for the Commodore Amiga written by Matt Dillon and his team, Obvious Implementations Corp. After the Commodore bankruptcy and Amiga's decline in the late 1990s, DICE ceased development and Matt and the team moved on to other projects.

In 2023 Matt released the DICE source code under the BSD License. The DICE-nx project made a fork of that DICE release and worked to fix it up again for the Amiga and produce new releases.

The new release is version 3.20.0. To download it and get started, [see our Getting Started guide]({{< ref "get-started.md" >}}).
